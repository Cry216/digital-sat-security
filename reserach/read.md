# Problem

Digital standardized tests may face security risks when test content
becomes known before later test administrations.

The goal of this research is not to study how test content is leaked,
but how test systems can reduce the usefulness and impact of compromised
content after a security failure occurs.

# Initial Research Question

How can digital standardized tests reduce cross-administration item
preknowledge risk while maintaining test quality and reasonable
item-bank costs?

# Possible Technical Direction

Build a simulation of a digital adaptive testing system and evaluate
security-aware item selection and rotation strategies.

# Goal

Develop and experimentally evaluate a defensive strategy that reduces
expected exposure to compromised items.
